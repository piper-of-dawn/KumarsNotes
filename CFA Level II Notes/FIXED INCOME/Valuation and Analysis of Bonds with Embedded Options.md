# Value Capped Floaters

> A three-year floating rate bond pays annual coupons of one-year The one-year, two-year, and three-year par yields are 2.500%, 3.000%, and 3.500%, respectively, and interest rate volatility is 10%. The value of the capped floater is?

==First determine whether the cap binds. If it does not, as in this case, select the answer equal to the bond's face value = 100.==

Given: FV = 100, Coupon Cap = 4%, Annual Coupons, π = 0.5, Maturity = 2 years

Current Spot rate = 2%
Possible rates in Year 1 = 6% (Up) and 2% (Down) 
Possible rates in Year 2 = 6% (Up) and 2% (Down) 
Possible rates in Year 3 = 8% (Up), 4% (Middle) and 2% (Down)

- At year three, the coupons are 104 at the up node, 104 at the middle node, and 102 at the down node. Their discounted values are 96.29 at the up node and 100 at the middle and down nodes, since the coupon equals the discount rate. 
- Average to 196.29 at the up node, roll that back to year 2, and add the year-two coupon of 4 (capped). At year two, the coupon also binds, so it is capped at 4 and discounted using the year-two coupon rate, producing 96.36. 
- The down node stays at 100. Rolling back to time zero gives the cap bond value of 98.21.
